## Team
Led by Will Lawrence, who has high-tier domain expertise as the former Product Lead for Facebook’s AML platform and builder of compliance infrastructure at Paxos. The team possesses deep technical and regulatory knowledge in financial crime operations.

## Product
An AI-native operations platform for bank back-offices. It utilizes AI agents to handle routine AML (Anti-Money Laundering) and KYC (Know Your Customer) tasks. Key features include a 'Trust Infrastructure' for model validation, citations for every output, and audit-ready reporting designed for regulated environments.

## Market
Targeting OCC, Fed, and FDIC regulated banks and fintechs. This is a massive, high-stakes market driven by heavy regulatory pressure and the high cost of manual compliance labor. It is investable now because LLMs have finally reached the reasoning capacity to handle complex document verification and pattern matching required for AML.

## Traction
Strong institutional signals: Y Combinator (S23) alum. Successfully raised a $4.8M seed round (Nov 2023) followed by a $15M Series A (May 2025 per source).

## Competitive Advantage
Their 'Trust Infrastructure' serves as a defensibility moat, providing the governance and auditability that regulated banks require—something generic LLM wrappers lack. The founder's pedigree in building Facebook's AML systems is difficult to replicate in under 3 months.

## Thesis Fit
Poor. While the product delivers 'autonomous value' (handling tasks vs. just assisting), it fails the core thesis of 'self-serve setup in under 15 minutes' for a non-technical buyer. Selling into regulated banks (OCC/FDIC) invariably requires security reviews, IT data integration, and lengthy sales cycles. It is not a 'no IT' solution.

## Risks
Sales cycle death: Regulated banks are notoriously slow to adopt new tech. Regulatory risk: If the 'Trust Infrastructure' fails an audit, the company loses its license to operate in this space. Competition: Legacy incumbents or well-resourced players could build similar agentic workflows if they solve the auditability hurdle.

## Confidence
High

## What Would Change My Mind
If the company could prove a 'Land and Expand' strategy where a branch manager or compliance officer could pilot the tool on a subset of data with zero IT intervention or API integration—truly hitting that 15-minute time-to-value.
