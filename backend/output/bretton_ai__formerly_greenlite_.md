## Team
Led by Will Lawrence, who has elite domain expertise as the former Product Lead for Facebook’s AML platform and a core infrastructure builder at Paxos. His background directly matches the problem space (combatting financial crime at scale).

## Product
AI agents that autonomously perform entry-level KYC and AML tasks, such as sanction reviews and investigations. The platform utilizes LLMs integrated with over 200 data sources to complete work typically handled by human compliance teams.

## Market
High-growth fintech and regulated banking compliance (AML/KYC/Sanctions). This is investable now because regulatory scrutiny is increasing while compliance costs are ballooning; AI agents offer a way to scale without linear headcount growth.

## Traction
Strong institutional validation including participation in YC (S23), a $4.8M Seed round (Nov 2023), and a $15M Series A (May 2025). Explicitly trusted by regulated banks and fintechs overseen by major agencies (OCC, FDIC, SEC).

## Competitive Advantage
Deep domain expertise and a significant moat created by 200+ data source integrations. The complexity of financial regulations and the need for high accuracy make this difficult to replicate by a generalist AI competitor within 3 months.

## Thesis Fit
Mixed. The product excels at delivering value autonomously (doing the work, not just assisting). However, it likely fails the '15-minute self-serve/no sales call' requirement. Regulated financial institutions (OCC/FDIC/SEC governed) rarely onboard mission-critical compliance software without extensive security reviews, sales cycles, and IT oversight.

## Risks
Regulatory liability: if an agent misses a sanctioned entity, the financial and legal consequences for the client are catastrophic. Integration friction: '15-minute setup' is extremely difficult in the banking sector due to legacy systems and data silos.
