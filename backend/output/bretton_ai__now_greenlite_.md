## Team
Led by Will Lawrence, who brings exceptional domain expertise. He previously led product for Facebook’s Anti-Money Laundering (AML) platform and built core compliance infrastructure at Paxos. His background directly aligns with the technical and regulatory challenges of the product.

## Product
An AI-native operations platform for banks and fintechs to handle compliance, risk, and fraud. It features a proprietary 'Trust Infrastructure' that provides model validation, governance, and auditability, ensuring every AI output is traced and cited for regulators (OCC, Fed, FDIC).

## Market
Large-scale opportunity within the financial back-office sector. Banks and fintechs face rising compliance costs and regulatory scrutiny. The 'investable now' catalyst is the maturity of LLMs combined with the specific need for 'audit-ready' AI that can handle regulated workflows autonomously.

## Traction
Very strong. The company raised a $4.8 million seed round in November 2023 and followed up with a $15 million Series A in May 2025. It is a Y Combinator (S23) alum.

## Competitive Advantage
The 'Trust Infrastructure' serves as a defensible moat. Unlike generic AI wrappers, Bretton focuses on the 'governance and auditability guardrails' required by federal regulators. This deep integration into the regulatory workflow and 'traceable' outputs makes it harder for generalist competitors to replicate within 3 months.

## Thesis Fit
Poor. While the product is 'autonomous' (it does the work of a compliance team rather than just assisting), it fails the self-serve/15-minute setup test. Regulated banks using their 'own data and policies' inevitably require complex data integrations, security reviews, and IT involvement. The nature of FDIC/Fed-regulated banking operations is fundamentally incompatible with a 'no sales call/no IT' friction-less onboarding.

## Risks
High friction in sales and implementation due to the heavy regulation of the target buyer; potential for regulatory shifts regarding AI-driven financial decisions; long enterprise sales cycles that could drain capital despite recent funding.
