## Team
Strong founder-market fit. Ahmed Gaber (CTO) brings 10+ years of fintech experience including Funding Circle. Edoardo Maschio (CEO) has a background in BCG and Rocket Internet, specifically incubating fintech ventures. They possess the necessary combination of technical execution and industry-specific business development experience.

## Product
AI agents designed to automate customer due diligence (CDD) and AML workflows. Specifically, it reviews registry extracts, proofs of address, and remediates false positive alerts. The product shifts from 'human-in-the-loop' to 'human-on-the-loop' by performing the investigative legwork autonomously.

## Market
Significant opportunity within Regtech. Fintechs and banks face rising 'cost-to-comply' and high false-positive rates in AML systems. LLMs are a timely catalyst, enabling the processing of unstructured data (legal documents, media) that previously required expensive manual labor.

## Traction
Raised $2.5M in seed funding (March 2024). Graduated YC Summer 2023. The product is active and being marketed to banks and fintechs, though specific customer counts and revenue are not disclosed.

## Competitive Advantage
Advantage stems from domain-specific fine-tuning for AML/KYC workflows and remediating false positives. However, defensibility is a concern as document-processing AI is a crowded space; the 'moat' depends on deep integration into the banking stack and regulatory-grade audit trails.

## Thesis Fit
Poor fit for our specific thesis. While the product is 'autonomous' (doing the work), the buyer is a Fintech/Bank compliance team—a cohort that typically requires lengthy security reviews, SOC2 compliance, and data integrations. A '15-minute self-serve setup for non-technical buyers' is virtually impossible in the highly regulated AML/KYC space.

## Risks
Sales cycle friction is the primary risk; banking procurement is the opposite of 'self-serve.' Additionally, the '3-month replication' risk is high as many well-resourced incumbents (e.g., ComplyAdvantage) are currently integrating LLMs into their existing data moats.

## Confidence
High

## What Would Change My Mind
If the company demonstrated a 'plug-and-play' browser extension or API that could analyze public registry data and news without requiring access to the bank's core internal database, potentially allowing a compliance officer to start using it before a full enterprise IT integration.
