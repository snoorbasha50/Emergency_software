## Team
The team is led by Angela Castillo, a second-time founder who previously raised $1.5M for Helloguru (no-code tool). Her tenure as Chief of Staff to the CEO at Drift is highly relevant, as Drift pioneered the conversational marketing space. This provides the team with deep domain expertise in sales automation and startup scaling. Felipe Abello is also a co-founder (though his specific background is truncated in the source).

## Product
Nara is an AI sales agent designed for SMB websites and social media. It functions autonomously to answer approximately 70% of customer support and sales inquiries. Unlike human-assist tools, it provides personalized shopping recommendations and operates 24/7 to augment a business's sales capacity without manual intervention.

## Market
The market targets small and medium businesses (SMBs) needing to scale sales and support. This is investable now because the cost of deploying high-quality LLM-based agents has dropped significantly, allowing SMBs to access technology previously reserved for enterprise-level budgets with large IT teams.

## Traction
Nara was part of the YC Winter 2022 batch. The most significant traction signal is its status as 'Acquired,' indicating that the team or technology reached a level of maturity and value that prompted an exit.

## Competitive Advantage
The advantage stems from the founders' background in no-code (Helloguru) and conversational sales (Drift), likely manifesting in a UX that is specifically optimized for SMB workflows. However, the core tech (AI chat) faces high replicability risks.

## Thesis Fit
Strong fit on autonomy and target buyer. The product 'does the work' by answering 70% of questions autonomously. It targets SMBs, implying a non-technical, self-serve setup. The primary tension is the '3-month replication' rule; as an AI agent for SMBs, it sits in a highly crowded space where defensibility is difficult unless the integration/workflow is uniquely sticky.

## Risks
The primary risk is commoditization. Large incumbents (Drift, Intercom) or well-resourced startups could replicate the core 'AI Agent' functionality within 3 months. Additionally, SMB customers are notorious for high churn if the product doesn't deliver immediate, measurable ROI.
