## Team
Led by CEO Pranay Shetty, who brings a background in investment banking, private equity, and fintech, alongside scaling experience as an early employee at CloudKitchens. Co-founded by Ramkumar Venkataraman. The team possesses strong domain expertise and experience in high-growth environments.

## Product
AI operations platform featuring agents that automate communication with borrowers and assist loan officers in the mortgage and banking sectors to reduce origination and servicing costs.

## Market
Mortgage lending and banking operations. This is investable now because lenders are facing high operational costs and margin pressure, creating an urgent need for AI-driven efficiency to replace manual labor in origination and servicing.

## Traction
The company is active and 'already live with banks, and top IMBs (Independent Mortgage Bankers),' indicating successful enterprise pilot-to-production transitions.

## Competitive Advantage
Vertical specialization in mortgage/banking workflows. While LLMs are a commodity, the specific integration into banking operations and borrower communication scripts provides a temporary head start, though its defensibility against well-resourced incumbents is a concern.

## Thesis Fit
Poor. While the agents 'do the work' (speaking to borrowers), the 'non-technical buyer self-serve in <15 mins' requirement is highly unlikely given the target market. Banking and mortgage industries require extensive security reviews, SOC2 compliance, and complex integrations with Loan Origination Systems (LOS), which contradicts the 'no IT, no sales call' mandate.

## Risks
Extremely long and manual enterprise sales cycles typical of the banking sector; technical debt associated with legacy banking systems; risk of being a 'thin wrapper' that a well-resourced incumbent (like ICE/Ellie Mae) could replicate in a few months.
