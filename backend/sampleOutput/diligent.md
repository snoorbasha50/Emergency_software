## Team

Led by Edoardo Maschio (ex-Rocket Internet, BCG) who has experience incubating and investing in fintech ventures. Co-founder Ahmed Gaber is listed, though his specific bio is truncated. The team has a strong background in operational execution and fintech incubation.

## Product

AI agents designed for fintech risk and AML compliance. The product autonomously reviews customer documents (registry extracts, proofs of address), performs due diligence on legal entities, and remediates false positive alerts from existing AML systems.

## Market

Fintech and Banking compliance (Regtech). This is investable now because LLMs have reached the maturity required to parse unstructured legal and financial documents, while compliance costs continue to rise for banks and fintech companies.

## Traction

Selected for YC Summer 2023. The company states that fintechs and banks are currently using the product to automate workflows.

## Competitive Advantage

Advantage stems from specialized workflow orchestration for customer due diligence (CDD). However, the source does not highlight a proprietary data moat or structural barrier that would prevent a well-resourced competitor from replicating the document-review agents within a short time period.

## Thesis Fit

Mixed. It excels at being an autonomous agent because it performs compliance work directly. However, it likely fails the 15-minute non-technical self-serve requirement because AML and banking compliance tools typically require integrations, security reviews, and compliance approvals.

## Risks

* Integration friction
* Regulatory and compliance risk
* AI hallucination risk
* Competition from established compliance vendors
